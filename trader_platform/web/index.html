TRANSPARENT PROFIT SHARING

10% of eligible net profit.

Your commission is calculated on positive net realized trading profit after exchange fees. Unrealized price changes and deposits do not count as profit.

  1. Reconcile a completed trade and its exchange fees.
  2. Carry forward losses. New commission accrues only above the trader’s prior cumulative profit high-water mark.
  3. Record the commission once for each unique settlement.

Previously accrued fees are not automatically refunded following later losses. New accrual resumes only after the previous profit high-water mark is exceeded. Collection remains disabled in preparation.
